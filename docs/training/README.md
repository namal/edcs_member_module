# EDCS complete user training

Open **index.html** in a modern browser, then press Play or choose a chapter. Keep the files in this folder together. No running Django server or sign-in is needed to watch the training.

- **EDCS_Complete_User_Training.mp4** — 1080p video with English synthetic narration, visible captions and 30 chapter markers.
- **EDCS_Training_Chapters.md** — timestamped chapter index.
- **EDCS_Training_Transcript.md** — searchable narration for all 129 guided steps.
- **EDCS_Complete_User_Training.srt / .vtt** — caption sidecars.
- **EDCS_Training_Poster.jpg** — video cover image.

The player supports chapter search, playback speed and fullscreen through the browser video controls. You can also open the MP4 directly in a compatible media player. If your browser restricts local video files, use your usual desktop video player and the timestamped index.

The tutorial uses actual application screens and step-by-step captures, with fictional training records in an isolated temporary database. It covers all 19 registered permission modules, user/role administration, district access, approval configuration and decisions, advanced administration, reporting and logout. It is a guided interface walkthrough rather than a continuous recording of live operations.

The examples include real saves within the demo: registration, plans, opening balances, transactions, child accounts, annual interest, loan issue/repayment/settlement, withdrawal release, officer handover, transfer and sequential approval. Rates and financial examples are illustrative; use approved organizational rules in actual work.

The screens reflect the reviewed application on 15 November 2026. Your menu and available actions depend on permissions, district assignments and configured hierarchies. No production records or outgoing emails were used. No application behavior was changed to produce the tutorial.
