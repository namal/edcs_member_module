Interest accumulates through the year and is credited once after year-end. A month before an account exists has a zero closing balance. A zero or negative quarterly minimum earns zero interest. Incomplete quarters remain pending. Amounts are rounded to two decimal places for each quarter.

The quarter containing a member's pension date or a child's rate-change birthday earns no interest. Other completed quarters are calculated normally. The pension rule applies to member Payment Types; child accounts use the child's own birthday.
